  moderation and mediation
- **Business analytics:** customer churn and retention, KPI dashboards, risk and volatility modelling
- **Data pipelines:** cleaning, reshaping, and automating reports in R, Python, and SQL
- **Methodology review:** study design, analysis plans, and reviews of theses and journal manuscripts
